Case Study

Muunto Launch Event Poster

A promotional poster concept created for Muunto, an adaptive tutoring platform. The goal was to design a visually engaging event poster that communicates hope, clarity, and educational transformation while making key event details easy to scan.

Project Type

Student visual design project · Event poster

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

Focus Areas

Illustration, visual hierarchy, typography, layout, and message clarity

My Role

Visual designer responsible for concept development, layout, and final design

Project Overview

Designing a poster that feels both informative and inspiring

Muunto is presented as an adaptive tutoring platform, so I wanted the poster to do more than announce an event. I wanted it to visually communicate the emotional shift from confusion and frustration to confidence and understanding.

The challenge was balancing expressive visuals with practical communication. The poster needed to feel modern and engaging while still making the platform name, event details, and message easy to read quickly.

Problem

Making educational information feel clear, human, and visually compelling

Event posters often become crowded with text or rely on generic visuals that do not create much emotional connection. For this project, I wanted to create something that would stand out visually while still feeling organized and easy to understand.

Because the topic centered on adaptive learning, it was important that the poster communicate both information and transformation. The design needed to show the idea that learning support can move a student from overwhelm toward confidence.

Design Goal

Communicate transformation through hierarchy, contrast, and illustration

Clear Information

Organize the event title, date, time, and location so viewers can scan the most important details quickly.

Strong Visual Story

Use imagery and composition to show a transition from frustration and confusion to confidence and growth.

Polished Aesthetic

Create a modern, technology-forward look that still feels human, approachable, and educational.

Process

Building the concept around emotional contrast

01

Concept Development

I began by identifying the message I wanted the poster to communicate: learning support should feel adaptive, empowering, and personal. From there, I centered the concept on a before-and-after emotional contrast to make the value of the platform more visible.

02

Layout and Hierarchy

I structured the poster so the viewer first sees the brand name, then the event type, followed by the date, location, and supporting details. This helped keep the information readable even within a visually rich composition.

03

Visual Refinement

I refined color, spacing, and typography to create contrast between the darker “struggle” side and the brighter “adapted” side. The final composition was designed to feel energetic, optimistic, and easy to understand from a distance.

Design Decisions

Key choices behind the final poster

Illustrated Contrast

I used two versions of the student figure to visually represent the difference between confusion and confidence. This helped the poster communicate the benefit of the platform without relying only on text.

Color and Lighting

Deep blues created a technology-forward backdrop, while the warmer glow around the final figure added optimism and emphasis. This color contrast supports the theme of growth and change.

Typography and Readability

I used a clear hierarchy with large branding, smaller supporting headings, and grouped event details so viewers could quickly identify the purpose of the event and when it takes place.

Final Design

The completed event poster

The final design combines branding, storytelling, and informational layout into a single promotional piece. My goal was to create a poster that attracts attention first through emotion and aesthetics, then supports understanding through clear organization.

Outcome

What this project demonstrates

Visual Communication

This project shows my ability to communicate a message through composition, hierarchy, and illustration rather than relying on decoration alone.

Brand Expression

It demonstrates how I can create a distinct visual tone for a concept while keeping the design cohesive and readable.

Intentional Aesthetics

It reflects my approach to visual design: making something polished and engaging while still supporting clarity and audience understanding.

Reflection

Balancing emotion and clarity

This project helped me strengthen my ability to use visual aesthetics intentionally. Rather than designing only for style, I focused on how illustration, hierarchy, and contrast could reinforce the message and improve communication.

It also reminded me that even a single-page poster benefits from UX thinking. The arrangement of information, the order of visual emphasis, and the ease of scanning all shape how effectively the design communicates with its audience.
